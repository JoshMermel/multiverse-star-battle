How to Play

Place exactly one star per row, column, and bold region on each board. Stars cannot touch each other, even diagonally.

The two boards always match — changes to one board take effect on the board one too.

Controls

  • Click — cycle empty → dot → star
  • Right-click — toggle star directly
  • Click and drag — paint dots across cells

Books

  • Daily — a new 7×7 puzzle each day
  • 6×6 / 8×8 / 12×12 — puzzles by size and difficulty
  • Lattice — each board has rotational or mirror symmetry
  • Twin — both boards are the same, one flipped or rotated
  • Kaleido — both Lattice and Twin
  • Voting Districts — all regions have equal area
  • Not Sudoku — the right board looks like a Sudoku grid, but the rules are still Star Battle — one star per row, column, and bold region
  • Not For Humans — puzzles so fiendishly difficult they were designed to defeat human solvers; attempt at your own peril
  • Signoff — a message from the creator
