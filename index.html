How to Play

Place exactly one star per row, column, and bold region on each board. Stars cannot touch each other, even diagonally.

The two boards always match — changes to one board take effect on the other board too.

Controls

  • Click — cycle empty → dot → star
  • Right-click — toggle star directly
  • Click and drag — paint dots across cells

Books

Use the Book button in the top bar to browse available puzzle collections — each book shows a description to help you choose.
